Ideal result checklist

Judge every draft against this list, taken from the refinement brief. A draft that breaks any line has failed, however good it looks at full size.

  • Recognizable: silhouette and spelling unchanged, instantly recognizable as the same mark.
  • Spacing: even, rhythmic optical spacing between every pair, not default font spacing.
  • Counters: open counters and apertures, crisp and legible at small sizes.
  • Terminals: unified terminal treatments, every letter ending intentional and consistent.
  • One cue at most: a single subtle ownable refinement, only if it helps without harming legibility.
  • No effects: no gradients, shadows, outlines, swashes, arrows, charts or icons added.
  • Vector clean: clean edges and consistent stroke logic, suitable for vector reconstruction.

Presentation rule from the brief: solid black on clean white, single line, generous clear space. That is why every tile stage is plain white, whatever the page theme.
